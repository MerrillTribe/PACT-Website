# About PACT

<p className="pact-about-intro">
  The <strong>Provo Auxiliary Communications Team (PACT)</strong> is a
  volunteer organization that works alongside local emergency
  management and public safety agencies during emergencies,
  major incidents, and community events. Founded in 2025,
  PACT provides auxiliary communication support when primary
  systems are overloaded or unavailable.
</p>

## Who We Are

The Provo Auxiliary Communications Team consists of local volunteers, many of whom are licensed amateur radio (HAM) operators. PACT members come from diverse professional and technical backgrounds and are united by a shared interest in community service and emergency preparedness.
