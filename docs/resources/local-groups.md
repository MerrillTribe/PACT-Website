# Local Groups

Utah County is home to several amateur radio clubs and volunteer emergency response organizations. These groups provide opportunities for radio operators to develop technical skills, participate in community activities, and support emergency communications throughout the region.

      <p>
        The Utah County Emergency Communication and Support (ECS) Team was formed in 1981 and is a volunteer organization made up of approximately 30 volunteers. It is overseen by the Emergency Services Division of the Sheriff's Office and supports emergency callouts and planned events throughout the county. Some calls to service include supporting Search and Rescue and assisting cities during their summer festivals (Orem Summer Fest, BYU's Stadium of Fire, and many others). :::note Orem/Provo Community Emergency Response Team (CERT)
The **Orem/Provo Community Emergency Response Team (CERT)** program provided volunteer training in disaster preparedness and basic response skills, including fire safety, light search and rescue, team organization, and disaster medical operations.

Orem and Provo operated a joint CERT program for a number of years to support community-level preparedness and response. Participation declined in 2019/2020, and the local program was subsequently mothballed.
:::
