These charts provide a simplified illustration of selected U.S. amateur frequency allocations. Category colors summarize emission types but do not identify every regulatory exception, regional restriction, operating condition, or power limitation. The chart should not be treated as an exhaustive statement of legal operating privileges. Verify current FCC regulations before transmitting.
