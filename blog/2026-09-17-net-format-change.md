The format of the PACT Weekly Training Net is staying the same, but the repeater used for the second portion of the net is changing.

The net will continue to be held every Wednesday at **2030 hrs (8:30 PM)** using the same two-part format.

## 1. Check-Ins

The net will begin with member check-ins on:

**439.800 MHz Simplex**

After check-ins are complete, participants will switch to the **Provo City Repeater** for the remainder of the net.

## 2. Announcements & Training

Announcements, traffic, and the weekly training topic will now take place on the Provo City Repeater instead of the '47 repeater:

**Provo City Repeater**  
**Output (RX):** 443.575 MHz  
**Input (TX):** 448.575 MHz  
**CTCSS:** 123.0 Hz

Members are encouraged to participate each Wednesday to practice radio procedures, stay informed, and build familiarity with PACT communications.

For complete net information and procedures, visit the [Weekly Training Net](/docs/training/weekly-training-net/) page.
