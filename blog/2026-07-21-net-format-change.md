The PACT Weekly Training Net has a new format!

The net will continue to be held every Wednesday at 2030 hrs (8:30 PM), but we will now use a two-part format.

## 1. Check-Ins

The net will begin with member check-ins on:

**439.800 MHz Simplex**

After check-ins are complete, participants will switch to the **'47 Repeater** for the remainder of the net.

## 2. Announcements & Training

The second portion of the net will include announcements, traffic, and a weekly training topic on the '47 Repeater:

**'47 Repeater**  
**RX:** 444.325 MHz  
**TX:** 449.325 MHz  
**CTCSS:** 100.0 Hz

Members are encouraged to participate each Wednesday to practice radio procedures, stay informed, and build familiarity with PACT communications.

For complete net information and procedures, visit the [Weekly Training Net](/docs/training/weekly-training-net/) page.
