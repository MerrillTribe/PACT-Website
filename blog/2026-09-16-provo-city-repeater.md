Provo City has installed a new amateur radio repeater at Provo City Hall. The repeater will be available for use beginning **September 17, 2026**.

PACT would like to thank Provo City for installing the repeater and making it available for all PACT operations and communications.

### Provo City Repeater

**Input (TX):** 448.575 MHz  
**Output (RX):** 443.575 MHz  
**CTCSS:** 123.0 Hz  
**Location:** Provo City Hall
